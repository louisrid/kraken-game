      How to play

      Every prompt has loads of right answers, like “Name a type of pasta”. Type one. If it’s valid, it sinks to a depth. The rarer it is, the deeper you dive. Deepest diver wins.

      Fool’s gold is the trap: the “obscure” answer every clever person reaches for.

      Versus

      • Everyone gets the same prompt and answers at the same time.
      • Wrong or misspelt? You’ll know straight away. Keep trying. No timer: the round ends when everyone’s locked in.
      • Answers stay sealed until the round ends.
      • Say the same thing as someone else and you’re both sunk: +0 m.
      • The last round counts double.

      Crew

      • The captain’s order lists the depths you need, like Shallows · Deep water · The abyss.
      • Anyone can answer, any time. An answer fills an open slot at its depth.
      • Right answer, wrong depth? That’s a strike. Three strikes and the order’s lost.
      • Miss an order and the kraken takes a plank. Three planks. Find the kraken’s pick for a spare.
